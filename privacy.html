Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sideline is an independent digital education project created and run by Zouphiel. This policy explains what happens when you visit this website or download the free Starter Guide.

Guide downloads and email addresses

The free guide is a direct PDF download. You do not need to provide an email address, create an account, or make a payment. Email signup and the course waitlist are not currently active. The website does not collect or store signup email addresses, and no form submission is sent to Formspree while signup is inactive.

Website hosting

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address and request logs, to operate and secure its service. This can happen whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

Sideline does not add analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party embedded content. It does not store your email address in your browser.

Product questions and public posts

Product questions can be posted on Sideline’s public questions page. Posts there are public and are handled by GitHub under its own privacy practices. Posting requires a GitHub account. Do not post email addresses, payment details, identity documents, or other private information.

If you have included personal information in a post, edit it to remove that information or request moderation in that thread without repeating the private details.

If email signup is introduced

Before email signup is activated, this policy will be updated to explain how email addresses are collected through Formspree, used, retained, and deleted. A private contact method for deletion and removal requests will be published at that time. Requesting a guide and joining the course waitlist will be separate choices; neither will authorise unrelated advertising or sale of your email address.

Questions and updates

For general questions about this policy, use Sideline’s public questions page without including personal information. Any material change to the website’s data handling will be reflected here.
